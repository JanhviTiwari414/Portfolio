About

Janhvi trained as an architect, earning her Bachelor of Architecture from Bharati Vidyapeeth's College of Architecture in Navi Mumbai before practicing across studios in India and Australia. That grounding in the built environment carried into a shift toward planning at a larger scale.

She completed her Master of Planning in Urban Planning at Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology, graduating with a Gold Medal. During her studies and internships, she worked on research including a guideline report for the Resilient Coastal Zone Development Plan, a project run with NIUA and UrbanShift (UNEP), alongside planning research for the Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority.

She now works on transit systems and urban mobility, moving from Graduate Urban-Transport Planner at WSP India into her current role as City Transit Expert for Google Maps at Cognizant.
